# Customer-churn-analytics-platform

## Project Overview

Customer churn is a critical challenge for subscription-based businesses because losing existing customers directly impacts revenue and growth. This project analyzes customer behavior using the Telco Customer Churn dataset to identify churn drivers, quantify revenue impact, and provide actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.

---

## Business Problem

Telecommunication companies face customer attrition, which leads to revenue loss and increased customer acquisition costs. Understanding why customers leave is essential for designing effective retention strategies.

This project aims to:

* Analyze customer churn patterns
* Identify factors influencing churn
* Measure revenue impact caused by churn
* Segment high-risk customers
* Provide data-driven business recommendations

## Key Findings

* Overall customer churn rate: **26.54%**
* Month-to-month contracts experienced the highest churn rates.
* Customers with tenure below 12 months were the most likely to churn.
* Higher monthly charges were associated with increased churn probability.
* Long-term customers demonstrated significantly stronger retention.
* Customer churn contributed to substantial monthly revenue loss.

---

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Promote Long-Term Contracts

Encourage customers to move from month-to-month contracts to annual plans through discounts and loyalty incentives.

### 2. Improve Early Customer Retention

Focus onboarding and engagement efforts during the first 12 months of the customer lifecycle.

### 3. Target High-Risk Customers

Develop retention campaigns for customers with high monthly charges and short tenure.

### 4. Monitor Churn Continuously

Track churn-related KPIs using dashboards and periodic reporting.
